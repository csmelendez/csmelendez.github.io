Interactive tool · 2026

MDR/IVDR medical device classifier

It guides you, question by question, through qualifying a product (whether it is a medical device and whether the MDR or the IVDR applies) and classifying its risk under the Annex VIII rules, with its EMDN category and conformity assessment route. Each result cites the article, rule and indent that determine it, and can be compared with the COM(2025) 1023 revision proposal.

  • 113 solved practice cases: qualification, MDR and IVDR
  • Copyable report with the full reasoning
  • Rules published as data (JSON), open to review and reuse
  • Runs in the browser, with no sign-up or cookies

Course: Quality and management in healthcare technology, Degree in Biomedical Engineering. Teaching prototype under testing (regulations checked on 14/10/2026). For teaching and guidance only: it does not replace the manufacturer’s classification. The tool is in Spanish.

Licences: code EUPL-1.2; cases, texts and rules as data CC BY 4.0.

Interactive tool · 2026

PICO evidence search builder

A six-step guide from a structured question to a reproducible search strategy: concepts, controlled vocabulary (MeSH, Emtree, CINAHL, DeCS) and free text, translated into each database’s syntax and documented with the PRISMA-S checklist. It builds the search strings; it does not run them or replace review by an information specialist.

  • PICO, diagnostic PICO, PECO, economic PICO and SPIDER frameworks
  • Syntax for PubMed, Embase, Cochrane, CINAHL, Scopus, Web of Science and BVS, plus trial registries
  • Five practice cases, including the proton therapy report and the transcatheter mitral valve
  • Runs in the browser, with no sign-up or cookies; progress is saved only in your browser

Course: Health Technology Assessment, Degree in Biomedical Engineering (Unit 1, evidence block: reviewing scientific evidence). Teaching prototype under testing. For guidance only: syntax must be checked on each platform. The tool is in Spanish.

Licences: code EUPL-1.2; cases and texts CC BY 4.0.

NewInteractive resources · 2026

Interactive resources on the vocational education and training curriculum

Six interactive pages to understand the Spanish vocational education and training (VET) system and its legal framework: the ladder of national and regional rules, how to check which text is in force, who decides each part of the curriculum, the path from a job to a qualification, the four dimensions of a VET programme and the figures for work-based learning. All follow the same guiding case, an intermediate-level Sound and Image programme taught in Castilla-La Mancha.

  • Sorting tasks and cases with instant feedback and the legal provision behind each answer
  • Equivalences between the Spanish qualifications frameworks (MECU, MECES) and the European Qualifications Framework
  • Designed to be projected in class and reviewed afterwards
  • Run in the browser, with no sign-up and no cookies

Course: Foundations and application of the curriculum for the services vocational families, Master’s Degree in Secondary Education Teaching (Services specialism). Regulations checked in October 2026. For teaching only: it does not replace the official gazettes (BOE, DOCM). The resources are in Spanish.

NewInteractive resources · 2026

Interactive resources on health technology assessment

Four interactive pages on the foundations of health technology assessment (HTA) and evidence review: who assesses, under which rules and in which phases; the steps of a systematic review, from the PICO-D question to GRADE certainty; a forest plot lab where students move the studies and see how the pooled effect and heterogeneity (I²) change; and a GRADE ladder that goes from each study’s risk of bias to the certainty of the body of evidence and the wording of the conclusion. All follow the same guiding case, the AQuAS and RedETS report on proton therapy (2024).

  • Animated scene-by-scene presentations, designed to be projected in class
  • A lab that reproduces the scenarios in the course notes and recalculates Q, I² and the pooled effect; risk of bias with RoB 2, ROBINS-I and QUADAS-2
  • European and Spanish framework: Regulation (EU) 2021/2282 and Royal Decree 415/2026
  • Run in the browser, with no sign-up and no cookies

Course: Health Technology Assessment, Degree in Biomedical Engineering (Unit 1: HTA foundations). Regulations checked in October 2026. The meta-analysis examples use fictitious data. The resources are in Spanish.
