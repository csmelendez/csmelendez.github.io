2009–2026 · Training and qualifications

National qualifications in clinical engineering

I sat on the Ministry of Education expert group that designed the Senior Technician in Clinical Electromedicine qualification (2014) and on the Public Employment Service panel that updated the professional certificates (2018). Since 2016 UCLM has taught modules of that programme in dual VET, under an agreement with the regional Education Department.

With: Ministry of Education, SEPE, Regional Government of Castilla-La Mancha, SESCAM and SEEIC.
